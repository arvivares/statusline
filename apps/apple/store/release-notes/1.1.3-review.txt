2. Purpose and audience
Statusline is a free, open-source utility for developers to view the remaining Codex and Google Antigravity quotas published by their own desktop Companion. It presents quota snapshots, reset times and sample age in an iPhone dashboard and Home Screen widgets. It is not a chatbot or content-generation service.

3. Setup and main features
There is no Statusline account registration, login, user-generated content, paid content, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase. No reviewer credentials or sample files are needed for the local demo.

Launch Statusline and tap View local demo / Ver demo local. This clearly marked fixed Codex example works offline and can be shown in the widget; it is not a live account or a simulation of notification delivery.

Optional live synchronization:
1. Install Statusline Companion from https://github.com/arvivares/statusline/releases/tag/v0.1.38 on macOS, Windows or Linux. Windows is an explicitly unsigned beta preview; macOS installers are Developer ID signed and notarized.
2. Use a compatible, authenticated Codex or Antigravity installation. Companion discovers available services without reading or transferring prompts, source code, conversations or vendor login credentials. Antigravity readings include Google models only.
3. In Companion Settings > Mobile sync, create a pairing. On iPhone, scan that private QR or paste the pairing link. No Statusline login is required.
4. Select a service below the main reading. The small widget follows that selection and the medium widget also shows the other available service. Only services published by that Companion are shown.
5. Keep the computer awake, Companion running and both devices connected. Open the iPhone app once after an update. WidgetKit controls actual widget scheduling; readings are timestamped snapshots, not guaranteed real-time.

Notifications are optional and independent of displaying quota. Enable Codex reset-credit alerts and/or quota alerts in Statusline, then grant the system notification permission. The quota option is separate and defaults off for existing users. After a real observed 0%, the Companion can report confirmed recovery of a short/weekly quota. A weekly unused-quota warning requires at least 20% remaining during the last hour before reset. Delivery and timing are best effort; no synthetic event or sample file is needed to use the app. Revoking either category preserves the other preference; disabling all categories removes its push registration.

4. External services and data
- OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity supply quota metadata through the user's local installation. Statusline does not generate AI content or transmit vendor credentials, prompts, code or conversations.
- Cloudflare Workers/D1 relay transports AES-256-GCM encrypted quota snapshots and credential hashes. The snapshot decryption key stays on paired devices. Notification routing separately stores an encrypted Firebase Installation ID, language and independent category preferences.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple APNs deliver opted-in notifications. Quota-alert payloads disclose the service, event/window kind and expiration; the weekly alert also implies the 20% threshold. They do not contain exact quota values or vendor/account identities. This minimal push metadata is not end-to-end encrypted like snapshots. Firebase Analytics and advertising tracking are not used.
- Apple camera APIs process the user-initiated QR scan on-device. Frames are not stored. WidgetKit/App Groups provide widgets/cache; Keychain stores pairing secrets.
