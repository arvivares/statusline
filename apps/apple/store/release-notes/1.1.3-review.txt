App Review information — iOS 1.1.3 (12)

The account holder tested build 12 through TestFlight and requests App Store distribution. This submission reuses that binary. Pairing, readings and widgets are preserved.

1. Recording
Retain the previously supplied physical-iPhone recording: launch, pairing, refresh and adding a widget. It predates this build and does not show the new notification settings. No real pairing QR should appear in public metadata.

2. Purpose and audience
A free, open-source utility for developers to view Codex and Google Antigravity quotas from their own desktop Companion: remaining quota, reset times and sample age in an iPhone dashboard and widgets. This is not a chatbot or content-generation service.

3. Setup and main features
No Statusline registration/login, user-generated content, paid content, ads, subscription or in-app purchase. No reviewer credentials or sample files are needed.

Launch and tap View local demo / Ver demo local. This labelled fixed Codex example works offline in the app/widget; it is not live data or a notification simulation.

Optional live synchronization:
1. Install Companion from https://github.com/arvivares/statusline/releases/tag/v0.1.38 on macOS, Windows or Linux. Windows is an unsigned beta preview; macOS is signed/notarized.
2. Use your own compatible signed-in Codex/Antigravity installation. Companion discovers services; Antigravity readings include Google models only.
3. In Settings > Mobile sync, create a pairing. Scan its private QR on iPhone or paste the link. No Statusline login.
4. Select a service below the main quota. The small widget follows it; the medium widget also shows other available services. Absent services stay hidden.
5. Keep the computer awake, Companion running and devices connected. Open the app once after updating. WidgetKit controls refresh timing; these are timestamped snapshots, not guaranteed real-time.

Notifications are optional. Enable reset-credit and/or quota alerts, then grant OS permission. Quota alerts default off for existing users. After observing a real 0%, Companion can report confirmed short/weekly recovery; unused-weekly alerts require at least 20% during the final hour. Timing/delivery is best effort. Categories are independent; disabling all removes the push registration.

4. External services and data
- OpenAI Codex/Google Antigravity provide local quota metadata. Vendor credentials, prompts, code and conversations are not transmitted.
- Cloudflare Workers/D1 transports AES-256-GCM snapshots and credential hashes; decryption keys stay on paired devices. Push routing stores an encrypted Firebase Installation ID, language and category preferences.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging/Apple APNs deliver opted-in alerts: service, event/window and expiration; weekly alerts imply the 20% threshold, not exact quota or account identity. This minimal push metadata is not end-to-end encrypted like snapshots. No Firebase Analytics or advertising tracking.
- Apple camera APIs scan on-device without storing frames; WidgetKit/App Groups handle widgets/cache and Keychain stores pairing secrets.

5. Regions
China mainland remains excluded. Free pricing and features are consistent elsewhere, subject to third-party availability.

6. Regulated industry and third-party material
No regulated-industry service or protected third-party content. Independent; not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI, Google or Anthropic. Names identify compatibility.
