Optional quota alerts help you know when you can use your coding agent again after exhausting its quota, or when at least 20% of your weekly quota remains during the final hour before its reset.

Codex reset-credit alerts have a separate setting. Both alert types are optional and require notification permission and a paired Companion running on an awake, connected computer.

Your existing pairing, readings and widgets are preserved. Notification delivery and widget refresh timing are best effort, not guaranteed real-time.
