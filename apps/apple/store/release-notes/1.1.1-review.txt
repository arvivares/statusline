App Review information — iOS 1.1.1 (10)

This update removes the manual quota/status-text editor. Live readings continue to come from the user's paired Statusline Companion. Existing pairing credentials and cached readings are preserved. The fixed, account-free Codex demo remains available when unpaired and cannot overwrite a paired account. Pasting a private pairing link is still supported as an alternative to scanning the QR.

Statusline displays Codex and Google Gemini quota from Antigravity; it is not a chatbot or content-generation app. Only services published by the paired Companion appear. Claude Code is currently Companion-only and is not presented as an iPhone feature. The English listing remains "Statusline: Agent Quota".

2. Purpose and audience
A free, open-source utility for developers to check remaining coding-agent quota, reset times and sample age away from the computer, in an iPhone dashboard and Home Screen widgets.

3. Setup and access
There is no Statusline registration, login, paid content, advertising, subscription or in-app purchase. No sample files or reviewer credentials are needed for the local demo.

Without external setup:
1. Launch Statusline and tap "View local demo" / "Ver demo local".
2. A fixed 70% Codex example is saved locally. Add the Statusline widget from the Home Screen. The demo is not live account data and does not simulate Antigravity.

Optional live sync:
1. Install Statusline Companion from https://github.com/arvivares/statusline/releases/tag/v0.1.28 on macOS, Windows or Linux.
2. Use your own compatible signed-in Codex and/or Antigravity installation. In Companion Settings > Services, check the detected services. Antigravity reports Google Gemini quotas only.
3. In Settings > Mobile sync, create a pairing. On iPhone, choose "Scan QR" / "Escanear QR", or paste the private pairing link.
4. Tap a service below the main quota to focus it. The small widget follows that selection; the medium widget also shows the other service.
5. Keep the computer awake and Companion running. The app and widget read encrypted samples independently. iOS chooses widget refresh timing; this is not guaranteed real-time. Open the app once after updating.

4. External services
- OpenAI Codex and Google Antigravity: quota metadata from the user's local authenticated installations. Service credentials, prompts, code and conversations are not sent to the phone or relay.
- Cloudflare Workers/D1, or a self-hosted relay: AES-256-GCM encrypted snapshots, credential hashes and operational timestamps. The relay has no decryption key or plaintext quota.
- Apple's camera APIs: user-initiated QR scanning on-device; camera frames are not stored.
- WidgetKit, App Groups and Keychain: widgets, local caches and pairing-secret storage.

5. Regions
China mainland remains excluded. Features and free pricing are consistent in enabled storefronts, subject to third-party service availability.

6. Regulated industry / third-party material
Statusline is not a regulated-industry service and does not distribute protected third-party content. It is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI or Google. Product names identify compatibility.
