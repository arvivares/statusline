Statusline 1.1.0 (9) — Antigravity on iPhone and widgets

Requires Statusline Companion 0.1.24 or later and a relay with services-v1 support. Existing pairings do not need a new QR. Antigravity credentials remain on your computer; only encrypted Google Gemini quota metadata is synced.

Please test:
1. Upgrade without disconnecting. Existing Codex readings should continue working.
2. With Antigravity installed and signed in on your computer, check that Gemini appears automatically. If Companion has only Codex, no Gemini placeholder should appear. Google-only installations should not display Codex.
3. Select Gemini or Codex in the watchlist. Verify the weekly and five-hour labels, percentages and reset times against Companion.
4. Keep your existing widgets. The small one follows the selected service; the medium one shows both when present. Leave Companion running with the computer awake and check a later widget refresh without opening the phone app. iOS controls the actual schedule; allow more than 30 minutes.
5. Check English/Spanish, offline sample age and recovery. A missing quota must not turn into 0% or 100%.
6. If you choose to disable Antigravity in Companion, confirm it disappears after sync while Codex and the pairing remain intact.

This build is for TestFlight, not an App Store rollout. Android continues displaying Codex. Send feedback with the app/build, OS version, visible services and sample times. Do not send private QR links, credentials or unreviewed logs.
