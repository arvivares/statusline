Statusline brings Codex and Google Gemini quota from Antigravity to your iPhone and Home Screen widgets. See remaining quota, reset times and sample age for the services available in your paired Companion. Pair by QR or private link with Statusline Companion on macOS, Windows or Linux to receive encrypted snapshots. The computer must be awake and Companion running; iOS controls widget refresh timing. A fixed local Codex demo is available without an account or computer. The iPhone app does not request agent credentials, API keys, prompts, code or conversation history. Statusline is free, open source and independent of OpenAI and Google.
