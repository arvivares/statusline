# Apple age-rating questionnaire

Answers submitted through Apple's current age-rating system on 2 September 2026. App Store Connect calculated **4+**, with the corresponding regional equivalents, in all 175 configured countries or regions.

## In-app controls and capabilities

- Parental controls: No
- Age assurance: No
- User-generated content: No
- Messaging or chat: No
- Advertising: No
- Unrestricted web access: No; the app only opens fixed Privacy, Support and project URLs.
- In-app purchases: No
- Loot boxes or randomized purchases: No

## Content descriptors

Set **None** or **Never** for violence, realistic violence, fear, profanity, crude humor, alcohol, tobacco, drugs, sexual content, nudity, medical or treatment information, contests, gambling, simulated gambling and chance-based activities.

The app displays only developer-tool quota metadata supplied locally by the user or their paired desktop Companion.
