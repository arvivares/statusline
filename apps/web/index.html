Help center
What exactly is Statusline?+

Statusline is an open source project that shows the quota for your local Codex session: how much you have left, when it resets and your short window. The companion lives on your desktop and can sync an encrypted summary to your phone and its widgets.

Do I need an API key or a new account?+

You don't need an API key or a Statusline account. For automatic sync, install Codex CLI on your computer, sign in with ChatGPT and pair the companion with your phone. On iPhone, you can also paste a /status quota line manually. The local demo works without a companion or an account.

Does the widget update in real time?+

The widget shows the latest synced sample, saved in the local cache. Update frequency depends on the companion, your connection and iOS or Android restrictions. Check the update timestamp to see how recent your quota reading is.

Does it work with other AI agents?+

Codex is the complete integration today. The roadmap includes an architecture for adding other agents, with AGY proposed as the first additional adapter and Claude Code and GitHub Copilot under research. These integrations aren't available yet.

Is this an official OpenAI product?+

No. Statusline is an independent project by Inmerzion. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by OpenAI. Codex, ChatGPT and OpenAI are trademarks of their respective owners.
