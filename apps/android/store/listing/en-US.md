# Statusline

## App name

Statusline

## Short description

Check your Codex quota and reset times from an Android home screen widget.

## Full description

See how much of your weekly Codex quota is left without returning to your computer. Statusline brings your remaining limit, next reset and latest synced status to your Android phone and home screen.

To see your own usage, install Statusline Companion on a Windows, Linux or macOS computer with a compatible signed-in Codex session. Pair your phone by scanning the Companion's QR code or pasting its one-time link. No separate Statusline account or OpenAI API key is required.

Your quota at a glance

• Remaining weekly quota, shown as a percentage and segmented bar.
• Next reset date and time in your local time zone.
• Last successful update and connection status in the app.
• A resizable Android home screen widget, with a compact 4 × 1 default on supported launchers.
• English and Spanish, selected from your system language. Other languages use English.

Try it before pairing

Explore the dashboard and widget with a clearly labeled local demo. No Codex account, computer or network connection is needed for the demo.

Private by design

The Companion encrypts usage snapshots on your computer before sending them through the relay. Your phone decrypts them locally; the relay does not receive the decryption key. Your Codex credentials, prompts, conversations and source code are not sent to your phone or the relay.

Statusline is free and open source, with no ads, subscription or in-app purchases. The project also documents relay self-hosting for compatible builds.

How updates work

The widget shows the latest status saved by the Android app. Open the app and refresh to retrieve the latest available snapshot and update the widget. New snapshots require the Companion to be running on an awake, connected computer. When either device is offline, the displayed information may be out of date. Statusline does not promise continuous real-time updates or increase your Codex limits.

Currently supports Codex. Other coding agents are not supported in this version.
